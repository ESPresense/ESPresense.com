## Notes

- **Check the revision before you pick an Ethernet Type.** It's printed next to the RJ45 jack. Rev 7 and later (RTL8201 PHY) use **wESP32 Rev7+** (`eth` 13), which is what the template sets. Earlier boards (LAN8720) use **WESP32** (`eth` 3): change `eth` to 3 before applying. Silicognition warns that the wrong type can drive pins against a LAN8720 and damage a pre-Rev 7 board.
- The board ships with MicroPython. Silicognition only replaces boards that are faulty under MicroPython, so check that Ethernet works before flashing ESPresense.
- There's no USB on the board. The wESP32-Prog / Prog-C adapters (CP2102N, auto-program) plug onto the J2 header and work with the browser installer. USB is isolated from Ethernet, so both can be plugged in at once.
- PoE is IEEE 802.3at Type 1, fully isolated (1500 Vrms). One mounting hole is at PoE potential, so don't screw it to a grounded metal case.
- There are no user LEDs or buttons.
- I2C is set to the Qwiic connector (Rev 8+: SDA 15, SCL 4). On older revisions those pins are on the header.
- GPIO 0, 16, 17, 19, 21, 22, 25, 26 and 27 belong to Ethernet. Keep GPIO 2 and 12 low or floating at boot.
