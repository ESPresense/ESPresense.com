## Notes

- **GPIO 15 powers the peripherals.** The WS2812 LEDs share a switched supply that stays off until GPIO 15 is high. The template sets it with an **Always High** output, the same way the [NanoC6](/nodes/nanoc6) handles its LED power pin. Without it the LEDs stay dark.
- **LED brightness is capped.** Eight LEDs at full white draw more than is comfortable on battery or a weak USB port, so the template sets `max_brightness` to 40, like the [Atom Matrix](/nodes/m5atom-matrix).
- **Battery level comes from the BQ27220 fuel gauge** on I2C bus 1 (the template sets **Battery gauge** to BQ27220). The node reports `batt` (%), `mV` and charging state, and Home Assistant gets Battery, Battery Voltage and Charging sensors. The gauge sits behind the GPIO 15 power switch, so it can take a few seconds after boot to show up. Needs firmware v5.0.0b5 or later.
- **Not used by ESPresense:** the screen, encoder rotation, CC1101 sub-GHz radio, PN532 NFC reader, IR, and the BQ25896 charger.
- The encoder button on GPIO 0 doubles as BOOT. Holding it during reset enters the bootloader, so don't hold it while powering on.
