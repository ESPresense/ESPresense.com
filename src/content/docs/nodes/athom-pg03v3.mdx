## Notes

- **The antenna is weak.** RSSI reads low next to a branded board at the same distance, which throws off fleet calibration. That's why it's listed with caveats.
- **The relay is driven as LED 1.** ESPresense has no relay output ([ESPresense#1316](https://github.com/ESPresense/ESPresense/issues/1316)), so the template sets the relay pin up as a PWM "LED" with MQTT control. Switch the outlet by publishing `{"state":"ON"}` or `{"state":"OFF"}` to `espresense/rooms/<room>/led_1/set`.
- The CSE7766 power meter isn't supported, so there's no power reading.
