## Notes

- **The antenna is weak.** RSSI reads low next to a branded board at the same distance, which throws off fleet calibration. That's why it's listed with caveats.
- **The relay is output 1.** It shows up in Home Assistant as a switch named Relay, and MQTT `espresense/rooms/<room>/output_1/set` takes `ON`, `OFF` or `TOGGLE`. The button is linked to it, so a press toggles the outlet, and after a power cut it comes back in its last state.
- **Power monitoring** uses the CSE7766, which is factory calibrated, so the only setting is its RX pin. The node reports power, voltage, current and energy every 10 s.
- **Install the `esp32c3-cdc` flavor.** The power meter talks on GPIO 20, the C3's serial console RX pin. The `-cdc` build moves the console to USB and frees that pin. Flashing over the serial pads still works the same way; you just won't see the serial log afterwards.
