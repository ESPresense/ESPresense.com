## Notes

- **The antenna is weak.** RSSI reads low next to a branded board at the same distance, which throws off fleet calibration. Hence the caveat.
- **The relay is output 1.** It shows in Home Assistant as a switch named Relay, and MQTT `espresense/rooms/<room>/output_1/set` takes `ON`, `OFF` or `TOGGLE`. The button toggles it, and after a power cut it returns in its last state.
- **Power monitoring** uses the CSE7766, factory calibrated, so the only setting is its RX pin. Reports power, voltage, current and energy every 10 s.
- Install the **`esp32c3-cdc`** flavor. The power meter is on GPIO 20, the C3's serial RX pin; `-cdc` moves the console to USB and frees it. Flashing over the serial pads is unchanged, you just lose the serial log.
