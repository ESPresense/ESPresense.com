## Notes

- Install the **`esp32s3-cdc`** flavor. It flashes over USB-C without pressing anything. The BOOT and RESET buttons are tiny and a case usually hides them.
- Opening the USB port resets the chip. A serial monitor that keeps reconnecting makes the board look like it's boot-looping; close it and check again.
- The orange LED is the user LED and shows status. The red LED is the battery-charge indicator and can't be controlled; with no battery fitted it flickers.
- Buy the plain XIAO ESP32S3. The listings also sell the C3 and the S3 Sense (with camera); pick the S3 option.
