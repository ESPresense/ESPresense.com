## Notes

- Install the **`esp32s3-cdc`** flavor. It flashes over USB-C without pressing anything: the flasher resets it into the bootloader.
- If the flasher gets no response, plug straight into the computer, not through a USB hub. Still stuck? Hold BOOT while plugging in. BOOT is tiny, and a case may hide it.
- Opening the USB port resets the chip. A serial monitor that keeps reconnecting makes the board look like it's boot-looping; close it and check again.
- The orange LED is the user LED and shows status. Without the template the generic build drives GPIO 2, which isn't an LED here.
- The red LED is the battery-charge indicator and can't be controlled. With no battery fitted it flickers or goes off; that doesn't mean the board is dead.
- Nothing else is wired: no button or I2C. D0–D10 are free for your own inputs, outputs or I2C.
- The firmware uses the standard 4 MB flash layout; the extra flash and PSRAM go unused, which is fine.
- Buy the plain XIAO ESP32S3. Listings also sell the C3 and the S3 Sense (camera); pick the S3.
