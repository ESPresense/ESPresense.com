## Notes

- The firmware calls this **LilyGO-T-ETH-POE** (`eth` 8), the board's old name. Both the original and V2 boards match it.
- There's no USB-serial chip: the USB-C port only supplies power. Flash it through LilyGO's downloader board on the 6-pin header, or any 3.3 V USB-TTL adapter wired to EN, TX, RX, GND and IO0. Without DTR/RTS, hold BOOT, tap RST, release BOOT.
- PoE is IEEE 802.3af and isolated.
- There's no user LED (only power and the jack's link LEDs), so the template turns the LEDs off.
- I2C is off by default. For a sensor, use SDA 32 / SCL 33 on the header.
- GPIO 18, 19, 21, 22, 23, 25, 26 and 27 belong to Ethernet (RMII and MDIO/MDC). Don't put sensors on them. GPIO 5 and 17 too.
- Other LilyGO Ethernet boards (T-POE-PRO, T-INTER-COM, T-ETH-Lite S3) wire Ethernet differently and aren't supported.
