## Notes

- **I2C buses.** The stick has three I2C connections but the firmware drives two. Bus 2 is the internal bus on GPIO 21/22, which the AXP192 power chip needs (the template sets **Gauge I2C bus** to Bus 2 and **Gauge chip** to AXP192, for battery level, voltage and charging). Bus 1 is the GROVE port on GPIO 32/33, ready for a GROVE add-on such as the [ENV IV](/addons/m5stack-env-iv). A HAT on the top header uses GPIO 0/26 instead: its add-on template, e.g. the [ENV II HAT](/addons/m5stack-env-ii-hat), moves bus 1 there, so you can use the GROVE port or a HAT, not both.
- **`leds` is empty on purpose.** Without it, the generic `esp32` build's default status LED on GPIO 2 drives the buzzer. The red LED on GPIO 10 (active low) can be added as a `pwm_inverted` LED; that pin is from M5Stack's docs and hasn't been checked with ESPresense.
- Both buttons have external pull-ups, so they use `floating_inverted`. Button B is checked on hardware; Button A's pin is from M5Stack's docs.
- The screen, IMU, RTC and IR emitter aren't used by ESPresense.
