## Notes

- The RGB LED (LED 1, Addressable GRB) shows status in colour. It's only powered while GPIO 19 is high, so the template adds an Always High output on 19 (no Home Assistant entity). The blue LED is LED 2, controlled over MQTT.
- GPIO 9 is also the boot strapping pin: holding the button while plugging in enters download mode.
