## Notes

- The RGB LED (LED 1, Addressable GRB) shows status in colour. It's only powered while GPIO 19 is high, so the template sets **LED power pin** to 19. The blue LED is LED 2, controlled over MQTT.
- GPIO 9 is also the boot strapping pin: holding the button while plugging in enters download mode.
