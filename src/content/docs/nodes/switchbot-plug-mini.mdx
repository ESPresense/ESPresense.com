## Flashing

[SwitchbOTA](https://github.com/kendallgoto/switchbota) replaces the stock firmware over WiFi, no disassembly needed. It works on stock firmware up to 1.4, and on 2025–2026 units that ship with v1.8 **as long as you don't accept the v2.x update in the SwitchBot app first**. It's unknown whether it works after v2.7. If the OTA route is closed, you'll need serial, and the case is welded shut.

## Notes

- **The relay is output 1.** It shows up in Home Assistant as a switch named Relay, and MQTT `espresense/rooms/<room>/output_1/set` takes `ON`, `OFF` or `TOGGLE`. The button is linked to it, so a press toggles the outlet, and after a power cut it comes back in its last state. The white LED follows the relay; the other LED shows status.
- **Power monitoring** uses the BL0937 with the calibration from [digiblur's ESPHome config](https://digiblur.com/wiki/devices/plugs/switchbot_15_amp_w1901400/) (`voltage_divider` 1450, `current_resistor` 0.0011). The node reports power, voltage, current and energy every 10 s. Check them against a plug-in meter. That config inverts SEL, and v5 has no SEL inversion setting, so if voltage and current come out swapped, that's why.
