## Flashing

[SwitchbOTA](https://github.com/kendallgoto/switchbota) replaces the stock firmware over WiFi, no disassembly needed. It works on stock firmware up to 1.4, and on 2025–2026 units that ship with v1.8 **as long as you don't accept the v2.x update in the SwitchBot app first**. It's unknown whether it works after v2.7. If the OTA route is closed, you'll need serial, and the case is welded shut.

## Notes

- **The relay is driven as LED 1.** ESPresense has no relay output ([ESPresense#1316](https://github.com/ESPresense/ESPresense/issues/1316)), so the template sets the relay pin up as a PWM "LED" with MQTT control. Switch the outlet by publishing `{"state":"ON"}` or `{"state":"OFF"}` to `espresense/rooms/<room>/led_1/set` — see [MQTT LED control](/configuration/hardware#mqtt-led-control-v40).
- The button publishes to `espresense/rooms/<room>/button_1`; it doesn't toggle the relay on its own. Use a Home Assistant automation if you want that.
- The BL0937 power meter isn't supported, so there's no power reading.
