## Notes

- Apply the [M5StickC Plus](/nodes/m5stickc-plus) board template first (v5.0.0b5 or later). The HAT plugs into the header on top of an M5StickC or M5StickC Plus, not the GROVE port. This template puts I2C bus 2 on the HAT header, so the GROVE port keeps bus 1 for another add-on, and the AXP192 power chip stays on the software bus 3.
- The template names `"chip": "esp32"` because it sets pins. Firmware before v5.0.0b3 requires a chip on every template anyway, so it works there too.
- The SHT30 publishes `temperature` and `humidity`; the BMP280 publishes `bmp280_temperature` and `bmp280_pressure`. Use the SHT30's temperature: in testing the BMP280 read about 1.5 °C warmer.
- The ENV III HAT pairs the SHT30 with a QMP6988, which has no driver. On that HAT, leave out the two `BMP280` settings and only the SHT30 reports (untested).
